# Lighting Match

Analyze direction, apparent source size, intensity, colour temperature, contrast and ambient fill separately for the original subject and environment. Compare highlights and shadow edges on the subject with illumination on nearby architecture and objects. Establish whether light is direct, bounced or mixed before grading.

Prefer environmental adjustment and restrained local integration over retouching the subject. Keep skin and fabric texture; avoid global cinematic grading or lighting effects without a photographic basis. Make only corrections supported by visible scene evidence and preserve an untouched source layer.
