# Shadows and Contact

A subject must appear grounded in the photographed space. Identify the exact ground-contact region and infer cast-shadow direction, softness and density from the environment's existing shadows. Match the shadow to light direction, source size, floor material and subject-to-ground distance. Consider contact occlusion, reflected light and nearby surface reflections where appropriate.

Do not add a generic drop shadow or cinematic effect. A shadow is not a substitute for correct scale, perspective or placement. If source evidence is insufficient, ask or keep the treatment conservative. Preserve original subject pixels wherever possible and keep generated shadow layers separate and editable.
