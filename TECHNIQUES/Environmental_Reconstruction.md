# Environmental Reconstruction

A reference photograph communicates place, architecture, materials, atmosphere and lighting; it is not automatically a reusable background plate. Analyze each source camera view, then reconstruct or select the same environment from a compatible perspective. Keep recurring architectural geometry and material appearance consistent while adapting viewpoint, horizon and visible spatial relationships.

When removing text, logos or other unwanted scene details, preserve surrounding structures and surface texture. Never leave prohibited signage legible in the final composition. Do not invent environmental elements unless required and supported by references. Keep all changes to the environment separate from the original subject whenever possible.

For Project 001 specifically, the user requested no gym name and the boxer outside the ring; see the project notes. These constraints do not imply that every future campaign excludes gym signage or rings.
