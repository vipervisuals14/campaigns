# TECHNIQUE — Environmental Reconstruction

**Purpose:** Rebuild a consistent physical environment for each camera angle without reusing a fixed plate.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-18

---

## 1. Core concepts

- **Environment bible** — the established geometry, materials, colours, key elements and lighting of a location.
- **Per-camera reconstruction** — the environment is re-derived for each photograph's perspective, not copied.
- **Consistent geometry** — lane lines, tile grids, architecture and background elements keep the same real-world relationships.
- **Perspective adaptation** — horizon height, vanishing points and texture scale adapt to the camera.

## 2. Establishing the environment

1. Study the environmental reference or approved composite.
2. Document the environment bible: geometry, materials, palette, key elements, lighting direction.
3. Identify which elements must remain consistent across the campaign.
4. Identify which elements may adapt (angle, cropping, background detail).

## 3. Reconstructing for a new camera angle

1. Determine the new photo's camera height, angle, lens and horizon.
2. Rebuild the ground plane and key geometry to that perspective.
3. Place background elements at consistent real-world positions.
4. Match lighting direction and colour temperature.
5. Match depth of field and atmospheric depth.

## 4. Rules

- Never force a photo into an incompatible perspective.
- Do not reuse the exact same background image for every photograph.
- Keep spatial relationships believable (scale, occlusion, depth order).
- Preserve photographic authenticity over stylization.

## 5. Common mistakes

| Symptom | Cause |
|---|---|
| "Same backdrop" look | Fixed plate reused at multiple angles |
| Inconsistent architecture | Environment bible not followed |
| Wrong scale | Spatial relationships not maintained |
| Flat/depthless background | Atmospheric depth and DoF ignored |

## 6. Verification

- Compare multiple composites side by side for environmental consistency.
- Check horizon and vanishing points across the set.
- Confirm lighting direction is consistent across all images of the campaign.
