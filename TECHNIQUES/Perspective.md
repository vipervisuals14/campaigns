# Perspective Matching

For each source capture, independently estimate camera height, camera orientation, focal-length character, horizon, vanishing directions and visible ground-plane orientation. Use architectural lines, floor joints, bag/locker verticals and subject scale as evidence; distinguish lens distortion from converging perspective.

Before placing a subject, check that the environment's apparent viewpoint is compatible with the source. Match the subject's scale and contact point to the floor plane, and preserve spatial relationships between foreground, subject and background. Do not force-fit a mismatched plate or reuse a background just because it came from the same shoot. Reconstruct or select a camera-compatible view of the same place. Record uncertain estimates rather than presenting them as facts.

No perspective measurements have yet been established as a reusable Viper Visuals benchmark.
