# Texture Preservation

Keep originals unchanged and work in separate, non-destructive layers/files. Retain native resolution, fine skin and fabric detail, photographic grain and natural edge softness. Avoid repeated lossy encoding, unnecessary resampling, denoising, smoothing, sharpen halos, invented texture and AI regeneration of correct source areas.

Use a high-quality matte that follows hair, clothing and limbs while respecting edge translucency and the source's original photographic softness. Edge cleanup should be limited to the matte transition; do not reshape the subject. Inspect at 100% and at final display size against the target background. Keep full-resolution exports and label drafts clearly; never replace original inputs or approved references.
