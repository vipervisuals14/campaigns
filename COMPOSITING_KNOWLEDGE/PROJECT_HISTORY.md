## Status definitions

- **Scaffolded** — folder structure created, no material yet.
- **In analysis** — source photographs under review.
- **In production** — composites being executed.
- **In review** — awaiting approval / feedback.
- **Approved** — final composites approved and documented.
- **Archived** — completed and stored.

---

## Per-project documentation

Each project lives in `PROJECTS/PROJECT_XXX/` with:

- `Original_Images/` — untouched source captures (never overwrite).
- `Environment_References/` — environmental reference images and the environment bible.
- `Approved_Composites/` — approved finals (never overwrite; new versions get new filenames).
- `Project_Notes.md` — analysis, plan, decisions, feedback, outcomes.
