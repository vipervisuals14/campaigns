# Visual Standards

## Global standards

- Target authentic professional photography, not a visibly generated or stylized result.
- Preserve original subject identity, pose, proportions, clothing, accessories, skin, hair and framing. Alter only what the composite requires, preferably the environment.
- Prioritize perspective and physical integration before cinematic polish. Match the subject's ground plane, relative scale, camera viewpoint, light and shadow behavior to the scene.
- Retain full source resolution, texture and grain; avoid unnecessary upscaling, denoising, smoothing, sharpening, HDR and repeated JPEG saves.
- Keep original inputs separate and untouched. Save work as a separate draft; obtain explicit approval before classifying an image as approved.
- Backgrounds should represent the same physical location and visual language without reusing a fixed plate where the source camera perspective differs.
