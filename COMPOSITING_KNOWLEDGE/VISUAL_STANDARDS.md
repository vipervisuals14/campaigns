# VISUAL STANDARDS

**Purpose:** The quality bar every Viper Visuals composite must meet before delivery.
**Last updated:** 2026-11-18

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Quality Requirements

### Resolution & Sharpness
- Work from the **original full-resolution** photograph.
- Output must match or exceed source resolution; never deliver an upscaled image presented as original quality.
- Preserve authentic photographic sharpness — no artificial sharpening halos.

### Texture
- Natural skin texture (pores, fine detail) must survive compositing.
- Fabric weave, stitching and material detail must remain intact.
- Environmental textures must be real or photorealistically reconstructed; no visibly "generated" texture.

### Grain & Noise
- Match grain structure between subject and environment.
- Never remove grain from the subject; never add mismatched grain to the environment.

### Colour & Tone
- Colour temperature and exposure must be consistent across subject and environment.
- No overprocessed colour, clipped highlights, or crushed shadows.
- No unnecessary HDR look.

### Lighting
- Light direction, intensity and quality must be physically believable.
- Contact shadows must exist where the subject meets the ground.
- Cast shadows must agree with the environment's light source.
- No cinematic stylization that breaks photographic authenticity.

### Depth of Field
- Background blur must match the original lens/camera behaviour.
- Subject must sit at the correct focal plane.

---

## 2. Strictly Avoid

- Plastic-looking skin or artificial smoothing.
- Excessive sharpening or HDR.
- Overprocessed colours.
- Artificially generated textures presented as real.
- Unnatural or unsupported shadows.
- Excessive image regeneration.
- Using a previously degraded AI-generated image as a new source.
- Unnecessary upscaling that invents detail.
- Reusing a fixed background plate without adapting it to the camera.

---

## 3. Delivery QC Checklist

Run against every composite before presenting:

- [ ] Original subject preserved exactly (face, identity, proportions, pose, clothing, hair).
- [ ] Perspective matches: horizon, vanishing points, ground plane.
- [ ] Environment geometry consistent with the project's established look.
- [ ] Lighting direction and colour temperature consistent.
- [ ] Contact and cast shadows correct.
- [ ] Depth of field and atmospheric depth consistent.
- [ ] Grain/noise matched.
- [ ] No edge artifacts (halos, fringes, cut-out edges).
- [ ] Resolution and sharpness preserved.
- [ ] Overall result reads as an authentic professional photograph.

---

## 4. Reference Classification

When documenting an approved image in `APPROVED_REFERENCES.md`, classify each observed quality:

- **Permanent preference** — applies to all future projects (e.g., "always preserve original grain").
- **Project-specific decision** — applies only to that campaign (e.g., "this campaign uses a warm grade").

Never elevate a project-specific decision to a permanent rule without explicit confirmation.
